# Chapter drafting prompt

Copy this prompt into the desktop assistant and fill in the brackets. Select only relevant scene notes or manuscript passage before generating.

```text
Help me draft or revise one bounded scene for my novel.

Project context (treat as canon only when stated as established fact):
[One or two relevant facts, or leave blank.]

Scene objective:
- Viewpoint and tense: [POV, tense]
- Where / when: [setting]
- Character's immediate goal: [goal]
- Resistance or complication: [conflict]
- What changes by the end: [turn]
- Desired tone / sensory focus: [tone]
- Approximate length: [length]

Instructions:
- Preserve the supplied names, facts, point of view, and tense.
- Do not resolve mysteries or invent major canon beyond the scene objective.
- Prefer specific actions, subtext, and concrete sensory details over explanation.
- Avoid generic metaphors, repeated beats, and summary where a scene is requested.
- Return only the requested prose. If key information is missing, ask a short question instead of filling it in.
```

## Other useful bounded requests

- **Scene diagnosis:** ?List the scene's goal, conflict, turn, and one continuity risk. Do not rewrite.?
- **Voice pass:** ?Revise this selected passage for shorter sentences and a more restrained tone. Preserve events and meaning.?
- **Options:** ?Give three distinct ways the character could respond next, with tradeoffs. Do not choose for me.?
- **Continuity:** ?Compare this excerpt only with the facts I supplied. List contradictions and quote the conflicting phrases; do not invent missing canon.?
